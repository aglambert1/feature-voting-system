# Landscape Opportunity Analysis: Concur Invoice

*Analysis based on 3 competitors*

## Executive Summary

The AP automation market has converged on four table stakes capabilities that Concur Invoice currently lacks: (1) Integrated payment execution, (2) Virtual card rebate programs, (3) Dynamic discounting automation, and (4) Supplier self-service portals. All three competitors (100%) have these features, representing critical competitive gaps. The market has moved beyond invoice automation to complete invoice-to-payment solutions that optimize working capital and turn AP into a profit center. Secondary gaps include payment scheduling intelligence (67% have it) and automated vendor onboarding. Frontier opportunities exist in multi-currency payments, community intelligence, and supply chain finance, but these are lower priority than closing table stakes gaps. The innovation whitespace centers on proactive supplier relationship management and collaborative cash flow optimization - moving beyond transactional portals to predictive, partnership-oriented supplier experiences. Recommended priority: Close the four table stakes gaps first (payment execution, virtual cards, dynamic discounting, supplier portal) before pursuing frontier innovations.

## 2. Feature Opportunities

### Integrated Payment Execution Platform

**Summary:** Native multi-modal payment capabilities (ACH, check, wire, virtual card) within Concur Invoice, eliminating need for separate payment provider integrations.

**User Value:** End-to-end visibility from invoice to payment confirmation, reduced vendor relationships, faster payment processing, and elimination of manual payment file creation

**Market Context:** Table Stakes - all 3 competitors (AvidXchange, Ramp AP, Coupa) have this. Critical competitive gap.

**Priority Score:** 0.98

**Competitors:** AvidXchange, Ramp AP, Coupa

**Evidence:**
> AvidXchange: Native payment execution within the platform using virtual cards, ACH, or checks without requiring external integrations
> Ramp AP: Supports ACH, physical checks, virtual cards, international wires, and same-day payments with vendor payment preference matching
> Coupa: Native payment execution with virtual cards, ACH, wire, and check generation directly from platform

### Virtual Card Rebate Program

**User Value:** Turn AP from cost center to profit center by capturing 1.5% rebates on payments, extend DPO while paying vendors on time, enhanced fraud protection with single-use cards

**Market Context:** Table Stakes - all 3 competitors offer this. Represents significant revenue opportunity for customers.

**Priority Score:** 0.95

**Competitors:** AvidXchange, Ramp AP, Coupa

**Evidence:**
> AvidXchange: Automated virtual card generation for supplier payments with rebate capture and enhanced security controls
> Ramp AP: Issues single-use virtual cards for vendor payments to earn 1.5% cashback while extending payment terms
> Coupa: Virtual card capabilities as part of Coupa Pay payment execution

## 3. High-Impact Gaps

### #1: Integrated Payment Execution Platform

**Market Gravity:** 100% of competitors have moved to integrated payment execution, making this a table stakes capability. Customers increasingly expect end-to-end invoice-to-payment in one platform. Without this, Concur Invoice is positioned as incomplete solution requiring additional vendor relationships and manual payment file management.

**Competitors:** AvidXchange, Ramp AP, Coupa

**User Demand Evidence:** All three competitors highlight payment execution as core value proposition. AvidXchange emphasizes 'end-to-end invoice-to-payment automation,' Ramp AP promotes 'multi-modal payment execution,' and Coupa offers 'Coupa Pay' as integrated capability. Current Concur users must integrate separate payment providers, creating disconnected workflows.

### #2: Virtual Card Rebate Program

**Market Gravity:** 100% of competitors offer virtual card payments with rebate capture, positioning AP as profit center rather than cost center. This represents direct ROI for customers (1.5% of payment volume) and is increasingly expected as standard capability. High switching motivation for customers leaving money on the table.

**Competitors:** AvidXchange, Ramp AP, Coupa

**User Demand Evidence:** Ramp AP explicitly markets '1.5% cashback' as key benefit. AvidXchange emphasizes 'rebate capture' in their virtual card program. The ability to turn AP into revenue generator (vs. pure cost center) is compelling CFO-level value proposition that Concur Invoice cannot currently deliver.

### #3: Dynamic Discounting Engine

**Market Gravity:** 100% of competitors automate early payment discount capture, representing 18-36% APR equivalent returns when captured. Manual tracking leads to missed discounts worth significant money. This is table stakes for AP automation platforms and directly impacts customer working capital optimization.

**Competitors:** AvidXchange, Ramp AP, Coupa

**User Demand Evidence:** Ramp AP 'automatically identifies 2/10 net 30 terms and calculates ROI vs. cash position.' AvidXchange offers 'configurable rules for taking advantage of supplier discounts.' Current Concur Invoice users report 'manually tracking early payment discount terms and struggling to consistently capture available discounts due to workflow complexity.'

### #4: Supplier Self-Service Portal

**Market Gravity:** 100% of competitors provide supplier portals, recognizing that AP efficiency depends on supplier enablement. Without this, AP teams spend 30-50% of time answering supplier inquiries about payment status, updating banking details, and managing onboarding. This is table stakes for reducing AP operational burden.

**Competitors:** AvidXchange, Ramp AP, Coupa

**User Demand Evidence:** Coupa has '6M+ suppliers' in their network. Ramp AP offers 'self-service vendor portal for W-9 collection, banking details, payment preferences.' Current pain point: 'AP teams spend significant time onboarding suppliers, collecting W-9s and payment details, and fielding supplier payment inquiries.'

### #5: Payment Scheduling Intelligence

**Market Gravity:** 67% of competitors (AvidXchange, Ramp AP) use AI to optimize payment timing, balancing cash flow, discount capture, and supplier relationships. This moves beyond manual payment scheduling to data-driven working capital optimization. Emerging as expected capability for modern AP platforms.

**Competitors:** AvidXchange, Ramp AP

**User Demand Evidence:** AvidXchange provides 'intelligent payment scheduling that optimizes cash flow while maintaining supplier relationships.' Ramp AP 'recommends optimal payment dates based on cash position, discount opportunities, and due dates.' Manual payment scheduling leaves money on table through suboptimal timing decisions.

## Innovation Whitespace

Supplier Payment Experience & Communication Gap: While all competitors have moved to supplier portals for self-service, there remains an unsolved problem around proactive supplier communication and payment predictability. Suppliers still experience uncertainty about payment timing, lack proactive notifications about payment delays or issues, and have limited visibility into payment forecasting. The current supplier portals are reactive (suppliers must check status) rather than proactive (platform anticipates supplier needs and communicates automatically). Additionally, none of the competitors have solved the 'last mile' problem of helping suppliers optimize their own cash flow based on payment timing intelligence - the relationship remains transactional rather than collaborative. An opportunity exists to transform supplier relationships from 'when will I get paid?' to 'intelligent payment partnership' where both buyer and supplier optimize working capital together through predictive analytics, proactive communication, and collaborative cash flow planning.
