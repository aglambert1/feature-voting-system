# Demo Interview Guide

## Purpose
Gather structured feedback during demos to validate PMF and decide whether to move to production.

## Before the Demo (2 min)

**Context questions:**
1. What's your role and what does your team look like?
2. What tools do you currently use for product planning? (Jira, Aha!, Productboard, etc.)

---

## After the Demo (10-15 min)

### Core Validation Questions

**1. Problem Validation**
> "How do you currently track what competitors are building?"

*Listen for:* Spreadsheets, manual monitoring, nothing, Crayon (enterprise). Validates the gap exists for them.

---

**2. Pain Intensity**
> "How often do you update your competitive analysis — and how painful is that process?"

*Listen for:* Frequency (quarterly = low urgency, monthly+ = higher). Pain language ("tedious," "never have time," "always out of date").

---

**3. Business Impact**
> "Have you ever lost a deal or been surprised by a competitor's feature you didn't know about?"

*Listen for:* Specific stories = real pain. "Not really" = may be nice-to-have.

---

**4. Synthesis Value (Differentiator)**
> "How useful would it be to see patterns across customer feedback, internal win/loss data, AND competitive gaps in one place?"

*Listen for:* Excitement vs. "I guess that's nice." This tests the cross-signal synthesis value.

---

**5. Workflow Fit**
> "Would you use this alongside [their current tool] or instead of it?"

*Listen for:* "Alongside" validates complement positioning. "Instead" suggests they want more (or are dissatisfied with current tool).

---

**6. Frequency of Use**
> "If you had this, how often would you realistically use it?"

*Listen for:* Weekly/monthly = good. "Before board meetings" or "occasionally" = lower engagement.

---

**7. Willingness to Pay**
> "If this saved you [X hours/month] on competitive analysis, what would that be worth to you or your team?"

*Listen for:* Actual numbers. Anchors: $50-100/month for individual, $200-500/month for team.

---

**8. Must-Have vs Nice-to-Have**
> "What would make this a must-have tool versus a nice-to-have?"

*Listen for:* Feature requests, integration needs, specific pain points. This reveals PMF levers.

---

### Role-Specific Questions

**For Sales Leadership / CRO:**
> "How do your reps currently get competitive intel before calls? Would battle cards generated from this be useful?"

*Listen for:* Current process (ask PM, outdated docs, wing it). Interest in sales enablement use case.

**For CS Leadership:**
> "Do you see patterns in churn or support tickets that relate to competitive gaps? How do you surface those to product today?"

*Listen for:* Feedback loop pain. Interest in internal feedback → product connection.

---

## Closing (2 min)

**9. Referral Signal**
> "Who else on your team or in your network would find this valuable?"

*Listen for:* Specific names = strong signal. "I'd have to think about it" = weaker.

---

**10. Next Steps**
> "Would you want to try this with your own product and competitors during beta?"

*Listen for:* Yes + enthusiasm = potential beta user. Hesitation = probe why.

---

## Scoring Framework

After each interview, rate:

| Dimension | Score (1-5) | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Problem exists for them | | |
| Pain is urgent (not "someday") | | |
| Would use regularly | | |
| Willing to pay | | |
| Enthusiastic (vs. polite) | | |

**Threshold to continue:** Average 3.5+ across 5+ interviews, with at least 2 people scoring 4+ on willingness to pay.

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- "We already have Crayon" → Hard to displace
- "My team would never adopt another tool" → Tool fatigue
- "Interesting, but not a priority right now" → Nice-to-have
- "I'd need to check with [procurement/security/IT]" → Enterprise friction
- Polite but no follow-up questions → Low engagement

## Green Flags

- Asks detailed questions about specific features
- Shares specific stories of competitive pain
- Asks about pricing unprompted
- Wants to try it immediately with their product
- Offers to introduce you to colleagues
